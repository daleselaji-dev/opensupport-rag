Project Goal

Target: Build an enterprise-grade RAG system that can demonstrate AI
Engineer / Enterprise AI Engineer capability in interviews.

The project should not be a simple “chat with PDF” demo.

Positioning: Adaptive Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence Platform

Core idea: A production-oriented enterprise knowledge system with: -
knowledge ingestion - intelligent retrieval - evaluation -
observability - continuous improvement

The intended interview narrative: “I designed and implemented a RAG
platform addressing enterprise problems around knowledge quality,
retrieval reliability, evaluation, and continuous optimization.”
